Welcome to the Laboratory of Microbial Ecophysiology.
We study the evolution and adaptation of acidophilic microorganisms
across extreme environmental gradients.

Our Research

We investigate microbial ecophysiology and evolution in extreme environments, focusing on acidophilic communities, metabolic adaptation, and the molecular mechanisms that allow life to thrive where few organisms can survive.

Our Team

We are a multidisciplinary group of scientists passionate about microbiology, evolution, and extreme environments. Our team combines molecular biology, bioinformatics, and field ecology to understand the limits of life.

Outreach

Science belongs to everyone. We are committed to communicating our research beyond the laboratory — through public talks, science writing, educational workshops, and community engagement. Bridging the gap between cutting-edge microbial science and society.
